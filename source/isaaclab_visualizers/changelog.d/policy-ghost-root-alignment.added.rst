* Added an "Align to Reference Root" checkbox to the Newton HUD's Policy Ghosts section. While it is on, each ghost is
  drawn with its root horizontally over the reference's root every frame, so robots that walk away from their start
  stay overlapped for close comparison of their movements.
* Policy Ghosts are now overlaid on the reference in the Newton window's OVRTX view too, with or without
  "Align to Reference Root". Ghost tint and opacity still apply to the OpenGL view only.
