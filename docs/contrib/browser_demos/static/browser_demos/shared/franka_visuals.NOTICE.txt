Franka Emika Panda visual geometry

Source: Isaac Lab's FrankaEmika/franka_panda.usda (Isaac 6.2 asset collection),
a MuJoCo USD conversion of the Menagerie MJX Panda model.
https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/main/franka_emika_panda
https://github.com/frankaemika/franka_ros/tree/develop/franka_description

The Menagerie Panda description and its meshes are released under Apache-2.0.
A copy is included in franka_visuals.LICENSE.txt.

Browser modifications: welded vertices, reduced large meshes to 1,600 faces,
transformed vertices into native link frames, omitted textures, and packed
float32 vertices / uint16 indices into franka_visuals.bin.
